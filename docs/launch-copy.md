## Blog draft

### SignalCalc: a free scientific calculator toolkit for students

SignalCalc started as an old calculator MVP. It is now a small, free toolkit for students who need to work through maths and science problems without fighting the tool itself.

The main calculator follows the familiar scientific layout: trigonometry, square roots, powers, logarithms, brackets, constants, degree/radian mode, and a clear numeric keypad. It also includes a few practical helpers for common schoolwork: density, speed, Ohm’s law, and quadratic roots.

The goal is simple: give students a useful answer while keeping the method visible. Each helper shows the formula in plain language, and the short tips encourage good habits such as checking units and signs before trusting a result.

SignalCalc is free to use, hosted online, and open source. There is no account, no subscription, and no unnecessary setup.
